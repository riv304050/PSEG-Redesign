1. Background & Context

The current PSE&G customer website (nj.pseg.com) has grown organically over time to support many use cases: billing, outages, safety education, energy efficiency, WorryFree services, business services, and community information. While comprehensive, the experience is navigation-heavy, content-dense, and requires customers to know where to go rather than simply what they want to do.

Customer research and behavioral data consistently show that most visits are task-driven and time-sensitive:

Pay a bill

Report or check an outage

Start/stop/move service

Get help paying a bill

Find safety or emergency information

This redesign introduces an Intelligent Front Door — a task-first, conversational, and personalized entry point that quickly routes customers to the right outcome while still allowing room for promotional, regulatory, and educational content.

2. Goals & Objectives

Primary Goals

Reduce time-to-task completion for top customer transactions

Decrease navigation friction and menu depth

Increase successful self-service completion rates

Improve customer satisfaction (CSAT) and digital containment

Create a scalable foundation for personalization and AI-assisted support

Secondary Goals

Increase adoption of digital tools (alerts, paperless billing, app)

Surface relevant programs (payment assistance, energy efficiency, WorryFree)

Support regulatory and safety communication requirements

3. Success Metrics (KPIs)

Average time to complete top 5 tasks

Search/chat resolution rate without escalation

Reduction in call center volume for digital-eligible tasks

Experience Metrics

CSAT / CES for digital journeys

5. Core Concept: Intelligent Front Door

Definition

The Intelligent Front Door is a redesigned landing experience that prioritizes:

Immediate access to core transactions

Conversational, intent-based search

Context-aware personalization

The landing page is not a content index — it is a decision accelerator.

6. Landing Page Requirements

6.1 Layout Principles

Minimal cognitive load

Clear visual hierarchy

Mobile-first design

Fast load performance

6.2 Above-the-Fold Content (Required)

A. Intelligent Search / Chat Entry (Primary CTA)

Prominent conversational input ("How can we help today?")

Accepts natural language (e.g., "power is out", "need help paying bill")

Supports both typed and suggested prompts

B. Core Transaction Shortcuts

Displayed as large, accessible action tiles:

Pay My Bill

Report / Check Outage

Start / Stop / Move Service

Get Help Paying My Bill

Emergencies & Safety

C. Alerts & Status Messaging

Real-time outage or storm alerts (geo-aware if permitted)

Account-specific alerts for logged-in users (bill due, service issue)

6.3 Promotional / Informational Zone (Secondary)

Utility-controlled messaging (regulatory, seasonal, programs)

Dynamic but constrained (no more than 25–30% of page height)

Rotational with governance rules

7. Intelligent Search / Conversational Assistant

7.1 Functional Requirements

Natural language understanding (NLU)

Intent detection (billing, outage, safety, service, fraud, etc.)

Ability to route users to:

Direct actions (deep links)

Guided flows

Relevant articles

7.2 Experience Principles

Task-first, not article-first

Progressive disclosure (ask follow-ups only when needed)

Clear confirmation of understanding ("Sounds like you want to…")

7.3 Example User Inputs

"My power went out"

"I can’t pay my bill"

"Move service next week"

"Is this text from PSE&G a scam?"

7.4 Escalation & Fallback

Offer live support or phone options when confidence is low

Always surface emergency paths immediately when detected
